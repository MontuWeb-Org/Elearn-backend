# **1. Executive Summary & Vision** 

The Courses E-Learning platform is a centralized, comprehensive educational ecosystem built specifically for independent educators, tutors, and instructors teaching IGCSE (IG) and American Diploma curricula. The platform replaces fragmented setups (such as scattered WhatsApp groups, disjointed Google Drives, separate Zoom links, and external quiz tools) with an all-in-one digital workspace that unifies students, parents, teaching assistants, and instructors. 

# **2. Target Audience & Market Focus** 

- **Primary Instructors:** IG and American curriculum teachers who manage multiple classes, private groups, and high-stakes exam preparation. 

- **Stakeholders Involved:** Teachers, students, parents (for monitoring progress and attendance), and teaching assistants (TAs) who grade assignments and manage day-to-day operations. 

# **3. Core Project Scope & Features** 

The platform delivers a complete end-to-end administrative and learning management solution structured into four primary pillars: 

### **A. Multi-Tier Account & Access Hierarchy** 

- **Instructor Dashboard:** Master control center to oversee courses, schedules, revenue, and overall student performance. 

- **Teaching Assistant (TA) Accounts:** Delegated access for TAs to manage attendance, grade quizzes, upload homework solutions, and assist with student support. 

- **Parent Portal:** Linked parent accounts providing transparency into attendance records, quiz grades, fee payment statuses, and upcoming session schedules. 

- **Student Portal:** Clean, focused interface for accessing learning materials, attending live classes, submitting assignments, and taking quizzes. 

### **B. Curriculum & Content Management** 

- **Course Structuring:** Modular organization by subjects, terms, chapters, and lessons. 

- **Rich Material Sharing:** Secure storage and distribution for PDF notes, video lectures, homework sheets, and reference files. 

- **Quizzes & Assessment Engine:** Built-in quiz creation tools supporting multiple-choice questions, structured answers, and automated grading where applicable. 

### **C. Session Management (Online & Offline)** 

4 



- **Unified Scheduling:** Centralized calendar displaying both physical classroom sessions and virtual classes. 

- **Online Class Integration:** Seamless embedding or linking of video conferencing tools (Zoom/Google Meet) with automated attendance tracking. 

- **Offline Session Tracking:** Location management and physical attendance logging. 

- **Real-Time Synchronization:** Instant reflection of session updates, schedule changes, and homework drops on the student's and parent's dashboard. 

# **4. Developer Personas & Empathy Guidelines** 

To ensure our engineering team builds an intuitive system, developers must keep these core user profiles in mind: 

### **Persona 1: Mr. Ahmed (The IG Physics Teacher)** 

- **Background:** Teaches over 150 students across different IG boards, balancing physical classes at a center with online revision sessions. 

- **Pain Points:** Drowning in WhatsApp messages from parents asking about grades; struggling to track who submitted homework across different sections; wasting time emailing PDF files. 

- **Developer Goal:** Provide a lightning-fast central hub where posting a quiz or updating a schedule takes seconds, and where automated parent-student linking eliminates manual administrative overhead. 

### **Persona 2: Parent Sara (The Concerned Mother)** 

- **Background:** Has two children taking American Diploma math and science courses and wants to ensure they are keeping up with assignments and attending classes. 

- **Pain Points:** Blind to whether her children actually attended their online sessions or completed their weekly quizzes until report cards come out. 

- **Developer Goal:** Design a transparent, mobile-friendly parent view with clear attendance badges and grade summaries that require zero technical effort to navigate.
